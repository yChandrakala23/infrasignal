InfraSignal
Bengaluru road problem reporting (student prototype, not an official government service)

Seen a pothole or broken road?

Report potholes, damaged roads, cracks, waterlogging damage, incomplete roadworks or recurring damage. Reports near each other are grouped so officers see one issue instead of many separate complaints.

Your contact details are optional and never shown publicly. Photos are visible only to monitoring officers, and location metadata is removed from them.
Or tap the map to place a pin.
Grouped issues (circles) and single reports (dots). Demo reports are labelled.
