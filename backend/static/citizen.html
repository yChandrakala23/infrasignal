About the data

InfraSignal puts three kinds of information on one map. They are kept apart so you can see how much to trust each one.

Government record A work order, tender or document published by a public body. A link to a road is marked verified only after an officer confirmed it.
Inferred Matched by the system from a road name and ward. It may be wrong and has not been confirmed.
Citizen-submitted What people reported. A cluster of reports shows a recurring complaint. It does not show who is responsible.
  • A road with no government record is still shown. "No record found" means none was loaded so far, not that nothing was done.
  • A payment record is never treated as proof that work was finished.
  • Reports marked SAMPLE are synthetic demonstration data, not real complaints.
  • Road shapes in the demonstration are approximate and hand-placed. Real centrelines can be loaded by an administrator.
  • Your contact details and photos are visible only to authorised staff.
